Bubble Sort: Paas wale 2 elements compare karo, bada hai to swap karo. Sabse bada last me chala jata hai.

bubbleSort(arr): for i in range(n): for j in range(n-i-1): if arr[j] > arr[j+1]: swap(arr[j], arr[j+1])
